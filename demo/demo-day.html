The ARK: paired demo

One partner plays the trainer, the other the student. After the first round, swap. About 25 minutes a round.

Before you start (each person, 5 minutes)

  • Open both demo addresses in Chrome on your phone, with internet, and add each to the home screen (⋮ → Add to Home screen).
  • Allow the camera when asked.
  • Know your college Google password (@rvnp.ac.ke).

One round, step by step

Together marks a step where the two of you work at the same moment.

Trainer

  1. Open the trainer app. Sign in with the pair's DEMO code and the PIN.
  2. Profile → Connect Google, with your college account. (Students' documents are shared with this address.)
  3. Att Register(s): choose your class and unit. Tap today's free slot, then Open register.
  4. Together Tap Show lesson QR. Hold it up for your partner to scan. Their name turns present.
  5. Together Close the QR. Tap Scan ID cards: everyone turns absent, then scan your partner's Student QR ID. They turn present again.
  6. Mark two other students Late and Excused. Tap Save.
  7. Report(s) → Marks: enter CAT 1 marks for a few students.
  8. Together POE tab: open your partner's CAT 1. Return to student with a note, for example "Page 2 is missing".
  9. Together When version 2 arrives, open it and Approve.
  10. Report(s): see the term register; Export Excel, then Submit to HOD.
  11. Optional: switch off Wi-Fi and data, open another slot and mark it. Switch back on: it is sent by itself.

Student

  1. Open the student app. Set up this device: choose the pair's class and any free name (if it says the name is taken, choose another).
  2. Together Check-in → Scan lesson QR: point the camera at your partner's lesson QR.
  3. Together Show your Student QR ID (top of the app) for your partner to scan.
  4. Evidence (POE): tap Connect Google Drive and sign in with your college account; allow what Google asks.
  5. Choose the unit, tap CAT1, and Take photo of a page of any written sheet. Drag the corners if needed, Keep and take the next page, take a second page.
  6. Tap Save and send.
  7. Together Submissions: see it With your trainer, then Returned with the note. Tap Scan again and send version 2.
  8. Together See it change to Approved.
  9. Open My attendance: search, filter and sort your lessons. Check your Google Drive: the file is in The ARK - POE.
  10. Optional: switch off Wi-Fi and data and scan a lesson QR. It is kept on the phone and sent later.

Swap

  1. The old trainer opens the student app on their own phone and sets it up with a different free name in the same class. (Their trainer app can stay signed in.)
  2. The old student opens the trainer app on their phone, signs in with the same DEMO code and PIN, and taps Profile → Connect Google with their own college account.
  3. Do the round again, using a new slot on today's timetable and CAT2 for the evidence.

Facilitator, on the projector (10 minutes)

  1. Sign in as DEMO01 (HOD): Manage → Registers to approve: open a submitted register and approve or return it. Department overview: who is behind. Absences.
  2. Manage → Student phones: reset a student's phone. Manage → Backup: the storage gauge and Back up now.
  3. Sign in as MIS: the term, the trainer loading, class lists, staff and PINs, and the Google Drive card.

Facilitator checklist

  • The day before: run python demo/demo.py reset --pin … --mis-pin … (fresh data with this week as week 4).
  • Hold the demo on a weekday (the timetable runs Monday to Friday) with working Wi-Fi.
  • In Google Cloud, the demo address https://rvnp-attendance-demo.ictpoe.workers.dev is listed under Authorised JavaScript origins.
  • Try one full round yourself first, on two phones.
  • Print this page, fill in the pairs, and tell everyone the PIN.
  • Phones used in an earlier demo: Profile → Erase this device in the trainer app first.
