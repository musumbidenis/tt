Everything you need on every phone

Mark a register in seconds

Pick the lesson on the weekly timetable and tap through the class. Doubles, CATs and streams taught together are handled, and it is saved on the phone at once.

Questions? We have answers.

Does it need internet?

No. Trainers mark registers and students check in with no network; everything is kept on the phone and sent automatically when the network returns.

Do we have to install anything?

No app store. Open the link in Chrome and add it to the home screen: staff use ark26.pages.dev, students ark26.pages.dev/student.

Can a student check in for a friend?

Each student phone is registered to one student. The lesson QR code changes every 20 seconds and the server checks every check-in, so old photos and forged codes are refused.

What if a student is on the wrong list or stream?

The trainer adds them from the register and marks them straight away. They show as pending until the MIS Officer (or the HOD, for a move between streams) approves the change.

Who can see the marks and registers?

Trainers see the classes and units they teach. The HOD and the MIS Officer see the whole department. Students see only their own attendance and evidence.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It runs on the college's own free Cloudflare and Google accounts, with no paid services.
