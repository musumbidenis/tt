# The ARK - design context

## Design Context

### Users
- **Trainers** mark lesson registers, show the lesson QR, approve POE evidence and enter marks - mostly on their own phones,
  in a classroom, between lessons, often with weak or no internet (the app works offline and syncs later).
- **HOD** approves term registers and marksheets, watches the department overview and absences.
- **MIS Officer** sets up terms, loading, class lists, staff and roles, and looks after Drive, backup and system health.
- **Internal / External Assessment Officers** run verification panels and samples.
- **Students** (separate student app) check in by scanning the lesson QR and send POE evidence from their phones.
- Devices are often cheap, low-end Android phones: small screens, slow processors, bright classroom light.

### Brand Personality
- **Calm, dependable, official.** It is the college's record of attendance, marks and evidence, so it must feel steady
  and trustworthy - never flashy, never playful at the cost of clarity.
- Voice: plain, friendly words in sentence case, no jargon, little information at a time (see TECHNICAL 11.5:
  " - " as the separator, short labels, short tab names such as ATT, "Department of Computing and Informatics" on documents).
- Emotional goals: confidence ("my register is saved and sent"), calm under pressure (marking 40 students in a minute),
  no anxiety about losing work.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Rooted in the RVNP crest: green field, red top plate, black RVNP box, white. Open to a full refresh of fonts, layout and
  components as long as it still clearly feels like RVNP's own system.
- Light and dark both (follows the phone's setting); light is the main context (classrooms, daylight).
- Anti-references: generic SaaS dashboards, glowing dark "AI" palettes, gradients, decorative motion, card-in-card clutter.

### Design Principles
1. **The record comes first.** Status (saved / sent / approved / locked) is always clear and never ambiguous.
2. **Fast on cheap phones.** Light pages, no heavy effects or web-font sprawl, big tap targets, readable in sunlight.
3. **Calm motion only.** Motion only for state changes, short and decelerating; everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
4. **Little at a time.** Short labels, progressive disclosure, one main action per screen.
5. **Official where it counts.** Printouts, exports and approvals look like college documents (stamp, name, QR).
